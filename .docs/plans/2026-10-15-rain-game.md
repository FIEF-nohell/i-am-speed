# Rain mini game

## Goal

Add "rain", a falling-words mini game at /rain/ where words speed up and grow harder over time and any wrong key resets the word being typed.

## Inputs

- Rules: `plan-execution`, `verification`, `docs-current-state-only` (no AGENTS change needed), `agent-docs-sync` (not triggered).
- Reused: `useCorpus`, `useSettings`, `useKeySound` (the "error" sound), `NeedsKeyboard`, `isFormTarget`, `keyLikeFromEvent` and `resolveKey`, `createRng`, `setFocusMode`.
- Learnings: none retrieved (the retrieval helper is broken on this machine, see the navbar plan).

## Design

- Pure engine (`src/features/rain/engine.ts`), no DOM, seeded rng, driven by `tick(dtSeconds)` and `press(char)`. A thin React shell runs requestAnimationFrame.
- Rules: the first correct letter locks the lowest falling word that starts with it. While locked only that word's next letter counts. A wrong key is a mistake: progress on the word resets to zero, the combo breaks, and backspace does nothing. Spawning never puts two words with the same first letter on screen, so the lock is never ambiguous. A word reaching the floor costs one of five lives. Escape ends the run.
- Difficulty is a pure function of elapsed seconds: fall speed rises smoothly (capped), the spawn interval shrinks (floored), and the word tier steps every 25 s: length 2 to 4 from the 500 commonest words, up to length 6 to 11 from rank 5000 to 20000.
- Score: 10 per letter, times a combo multiplier (one step per 5 clean words, max x4). Best score in its own localStorage key (try/catch), not in the attempts store, because accuracy-based stats do not apply (errors reset instead of accumulating).
- Language follows the lessons language setting. Lowercase letters only; input compares case-insensitively.
